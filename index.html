Why Primaxs

  • 1975tanko established
  • EXCLUSIVEMalaysia distributor
  • 11product categories
  • 1,700+SKUs catalogued

Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd is the exclusive Malaysia retail distributor for Tanko Enterprise Co., Ltd., the Taiwan industrial storage manufacturer established in 1975. Buying through the local distributor means Malaysian stock and Ringgit pricing, help selecting the right model from a technician who has physically handled the range, and after-sales support routed through a Malaysia office instead of an overseas RMA queue. Bulk orders, custom configurations and site delivery for factories, workshops, laboratories and campuses across Peninsular and East Malaysia are handled through our team directly.
